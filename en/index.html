Pati — Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 23, 2026
Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Pati (the "App", "we", "us" or "our") has been developed to make care, health and daily tracking easier for pet owners. This Privacy Policy explains what data is collected when you use our app, how it is used and how it is protected.

1. Information Collected

Account Information

  • Email address
  • Sign in with Apple credentials
  • Anonymous user ID

Pet Information

  • Name, species, breed, sex, date of birth
  • Health information, weight records
  • Vaccination and veterinary records
  • Care data entered by the user

User Content

  • Uploaded photos
  • AI chat history
  • Check-in records, notes and reminders

Technical Information

  • App version, operating system information
  • Error logs, device ID (when necessary)
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA / Google Advertising ID) and in-app event data (install, session, purchase) — for ad measurement purposes

Location Information

The App processes location information only when you use the relevant feature and with your permission. Location data is used for the following purposes:

  • Nearby vet search — your device location is used to list the clinics closest to you.
  • Weather — to show weather information for your area.
  • Walk and activity tracking — to calculate walk duration and distance, only while tracking is on.
  • Lost pet post — to add the location where your pet went missing to the post, only if you create one.

Location permission is not required; if you do not grant it, only the features above will not work, and the rest of the app can be used normally. You can revoke the permission at any time from your device settings. Location data is not used for advertising purposes and is not sold to third parties.

2. Use of Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To store pet records
  • To provide AI-powered analyses
  • To send reminders and notifications
  • To verify Premium subscriptions
  • To synchronize your data across devices
  • To improve service quality

3. AI Services

The Neo AI and image analysis features offered within Pati may securely transmit text and images provided by the user to third-party artificial intelligence services for the purpose of analyzing them. These analyses are used solely to fulfill your request. Information provided by Neo does not replace professional veterinary advice.

4. Cloud Synchronization

For Premium users, data may be stored on secure cloud infrastructure and synchronized across devices.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Supabase (authentication and data storage)
  • RevenueCat (subscription management)
  • Apple App Store (payment processing)
  • Artificial intelligence service providers
  • Analytics services
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook SDK) — ad measurement, install/event attribution and campaign performance

These services' own privacy policies may apply.

Advertising and measurement: Pati uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure the performance of advertising campaigns and to improve the app. In this context, your advertising identifier (IDFA / Google Advertising ID) and your in-app event data (install, session, purchase) may be shared with Meta. If you do not want to be personally tracked: on iOS, you can decline permission in the tracking permission prompt shown when the app is first opened; on Android, you can turn off ad personalization or reset your advertising ID in your device settings. For details, you can refer to Meta's Privacy Policy.

6. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational security measures are applied to protect your data. However, no method of data transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

7. Account Deletion

Users can request the deletion of their account and associated data by using the "Delete Account" option within the app. Data is deleted, except for information that must be retained under legal obligations.

8. Children's Privacy

Pati is not a service directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. In the event of significant changes, notice may be given within the app or through appropriate channels.

10. Contact

For your questions: patiidestekk@gmail.com
